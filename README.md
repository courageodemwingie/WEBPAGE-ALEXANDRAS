# Alexandra's Floral

> One bouquet at a time.

A modern, responsive website for **Alexandra's Floral**, a Nigerian floral brand serving customers across **Ibadan, Lagos and Abuja**.

The website is designed as a digital showroom and conversion-focused storefront, allowing visitors to explore floral collections, learn about the brand, view delivery locations, contact the business and place orders through WhatsApp.

# Project Overview

Alexandra's Floral is a Nigerian floral business offering thoughtfully arranged flowers and gifting products.

The website is intentionally designed to feel:

- Modern
- Elegant
- Minimal
- Warm
- Premium
- Accessible
- Human
- Contemporary

The site avoids an overly corporate or template-like appearance.

Its primary purpose is to help visitors:

1. Discover Alexandra's Floral.
2. Browse available floral collections.
3. Understand available price points.
4. See where the business delivers.
5. Learn about the founder and brand philosophy.
6. Contact Alexandra's Floral.
7. Move from browsing to ordering through WhatsApp.

# Project Goals

The website has five primary goals:

### 1. Product discovery

Visitors should quickly understand what Alexandra's Floral offers and browse the available collections.

### 2. Brand positioning

The website should communicate an accessible form of modern floral luxury.

### 3. Conversion

The primary conversion action is moving visitors toward WhatsApp ordering.

### 4. Trust

The website introduces the founder and communicates the philosophy behind the brand.

### 5. Accessibility

The site should work well across:

- Desktop
- Laptop
- Tablet
- Mobile
